### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Software Engineer** and **Full Stack Developer** graduated from the Universidad Tecnológica de la Huasteca Hidalguense (UTHH). I am passionate about solving complex business problems by translating them into clean, efficient, and scalable code.

My approach goes beyond creating isolated applications; I specialize in building **complete technological ecosystems**. From managing **Linux** servers and databases to developing dynamic web interfaces, cross-platform mobile apps, **payment gateway** integrations (Stripe, PayPal), and automation with **Artificial Intelligence** and Machine Learning.

I have a constant hunger to learn new tools and technologies. I work strongly under **agile methodologies** to ensure continuous delivery that brings real value to end-users, seamlessly connecting web platforms, mobile devices, and IoT environments (like Wearables, Arduino, and Alexa).
